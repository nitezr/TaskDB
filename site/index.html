Your data stays yours

TaskDB talks straight to Notion from your Mac. There is no TaskDB server, no account, and nothing to trust but Notion itself.

  1. Connect Notion. Paste a Notion integration key — it’s stored in your Mac’s Keychain.
  2. Pick a page. TaskDB creates “TaskDB Tasks” and “TaskDB Projects” databases there.
  3. Work at full speed. Everything opens instantly from a local copy and syncs to Notion in the background — even offline.
  4. Use Notion too. Edit tasks in Notion, build your own views, link them to notes. Changes flow back to TaskDB within seconds.

Questions

Do I need a paid Notion plan?

No. TaskDB works with free Notion workspaces.

Where are my tasks stored?

In two ordinary Notion databases in your workspace, plus a local cache on your Mac so the app is instant and works offline. TaskDB has no servers.

What happens when my year ends?

Renew to keep editing. If you don’t, TaskDB turns read-only — and all your tasks are still in Notion, where you can keep using them.

Can I use my existing Notion task database?

Not yet. TaskDB creates its own databases so it can guarantee sync works. Importing existing tasks is on the roadmap.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet — use Notion’s mobile app on the go; everything syncs. A TaskDB mobile app is planned.

Windows?

Mac first. Windows is planned for a later version.
