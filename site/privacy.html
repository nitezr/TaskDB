TaskDB Privacy Policy

Draft — review before publishing.

What TaskDB stores

  • Your tasks and projects live in your own Notion workspace. TaskDB keeps a local copy on your Mac so it works offline.
  • Your Notion access token and license key are stored in your Mac's Keychain.

What we receive

  • TaskDB has no servers that store your data. The app talks directly to Notion.
  • When you use “Connect with Notion”, a small sign-in service exchanges Notion's login code for an access token and passes it straight to the app on your Mac. It does not store tokens or task content.
  • License checks go to Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider, and include your license key.

What we never do

  • We don't read, sell or share your tasks or notes.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking inside the app.

Removing access

Disconnect in TaskDB → Settings, or remove TaskDB from Notion → Settings → Connections at any time.
